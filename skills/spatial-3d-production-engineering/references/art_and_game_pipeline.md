# Drawing studies, Blender production and engine validation

Read the [Daftar game art research plan](https://github.com/occult-kranti/engineering-intelligence-atlas/blob/main/projects/game-development-research/README.md)
and the linked dated source audits before adopting a new tutorial, add-on or asset
library. Use its [drawing practice guide](https://github.com/occult-kranti/engineering-intelligence-atlas/blob/main/projects/game-development-research/ART_FUNDAMENTALS.md)
for original perspective, anatomy and gesture studies, and its
[pipeline matrix](https://github.com/occult-kranti/engineering-intelligence-atlas/blob/main/projects/game-development-research/PIPELINE_AND_RENDERING.md)
for Unity, Unreal, Godot and rendering workflows.

## Apply the reference to a concrete asset

1. Choose visual communication, real-time gameplay, offline rendering or measured
   capture as the intended result. Record the target engine, version and device.
2. Use Practice Drawing This for observation and original drawing exercises;
   preserve its restrictions on scraping/rehosting source reference assets.
3. Select verified Grant Abbitt/CG Cookie material for modeling, and verified
   Polyfjord, Ryan King Art or Ducky 3D material for the actual topic needed.
   Exact tutorial titles or repositories absent from the audit stay unverified.
4. Make one editable prop, node system, rig or lighting study. Record source
   rights, units, topology/UV/material decisions and reproducible parameters.
5. Evaluate Poly Haven or Kenney items individually. Optional MACHIN3tools must
   pass version/license/access review; do not buy or install it just because it
   appears in a reference list.
6. Choose interchange by receiver and asset class. Verify FBX/glTF import,
   compression-extension support, hierarchy, scale, normals, materials, skinning
   and animation with an actual small probe. Keep a decoded or simplified asset
   separate from its source master.
7. Use Robin Squares/stache/InLightVFX material for the verified rendering or
   compositing exercise. Record color management and passes. Do not treat offline
   rendering quality as target-device frame-rate evidence.
8. Return the source and derivative hashes, exact commands/settings, previews,
   engine results and measured limits. Label unexecuted checks as unexecuted.

Archived MB-Lab and Blender add-on repositories are research references until a
specific version combination is tested. The current official documentation in
the audit takes priority over a social post's blanket export recommendation.

For architecture, preserve the distinction between original game art and actual
site geometry. The [captured-place library](https://github.com/occult-kranti/engineering-intelligence-atlas/blob/main/projects/architecture-library/site-models/README.md)
records preserving web meshes, altered preview imports and unknown metric scale.
No added polygons, textures or generated ornament can establish missing measured
detail. The project accuracy targets remain conditional requirements.
