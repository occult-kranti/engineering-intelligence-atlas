## Research method

For every tradition, identify geography, period, patronage/community, materials/construction, surviving evidence, textual sources, translations, later interpretation, and uncertainty. Use primary texts/archaeology, peer-reviewed scholarship, museum/institutional records, and current conservation guidance. Separate measured geometry and construction practice from cosmology, symbolism, and later design revival. Do not use visual similarity alone to claim transmission or common origin.

## Tradition map

| Tradition | Evidence and topics | Starting sources | Common failure to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Modern/contemporary | Function, industrialization, materials, urban/social context, sustainability, accessibility, building systems, procurement and life cycle | Frampton, Modern Architecture: A Critical History; CCA https://www.cca.qc.ca/; RIBA process https://www.riba.com/work/finance-and-business-management/plan-of-work | Treating modernism as a universal neutral style or ignoring local/professional context |
| Classical Greek/Roman and later revivals | Orders, proportion, entasis, construction, ancient evidence, Renaissance codification, archaeological record | Vitruvius; ICAA curriculum https://www.classicalarchitecture.org/; museum/university collections | Treating Palladio/Renaissance rules as unchanged ancient practice |
| Ancient Indian and later traditions | Śulba Sūtra altar geometry; Vastu textual/cosmological layers; Shilpa practice; sthapati/guilds; regional/dynastic/religious diversity | AIIS https://www.aiis.ind.in/; Meister/Hardy/Michell scholarship; ASI https://asi.nic.in/; INTACH https://www.intach.org/ | Homogenizing Nagara/Dravida/Vesara or Hindu/Buddhist/Jain contexts; asserting uncertain dates as settled |
| Ancient Egyptian/pyramid | Surveying, quarrying, transport, labor organization, settlement/evidence, materials, chronology, religious/funerary context | Digital Giza https://giza.fas.harvard.edu/; AERA https://www.aeraweb.org/publications/; Lehner/Hawass/Tallet/Arnold scholarship | Aliens, slave-labor simplifications, geopolymer/Orion claims presented as consensus, or geometry without archaeological evidence |
| Historical Chinese | Timber frame, cai-fen modular system, dougong brackets, rank/regulation, regional/dynastic change, ritual/urban context | Yingzao Fashi research https://doi.org/10.1017/S0959133100000552; Palace Museum https://www.dpm.org.cn/; Liang Sicheng/Lin Huiyin scholarship | Reducing architecture to roof silhouette or treating one manual as timeless universal practice |
| Computational/parametric | Design-space representation, geometric rationalization, structure/fabrication, optimization, openBIM and information management | Pottmann et al., Architectural Geometry; buildingSMART https://www.buildingsmart.org/standards/; CGAL/geometry resources | Aesthetic output without constraints, tolerance, structure, information, or fabrication validation |

For additional cultures, build an equivalent evidence map rather than forcing them into the categories above. Include indigenous/community authorship and contemporary interpretations where relevant.

## Computational and modern practice

Use RIBA/AIA or local stage models as adaptable communication structures, not as a replacement for legal/professional scope. For technical delivery record information requirements, model purpose, units/CRS, LOD/LOIN where used, exchange formats, validation rules, issue tracking, and handover ownership. ISO 19650 and buildingSMART resources are starting points; current project contracts and jurisdiction determine actual obligations.
