# Heritage research to game-asset handoff

Use the [captured-place collection](https://github.com/occult-kranti/engineering-intelligence-atlas/blob/main/projects/architecture-library/site-models/README.md)
and [accuracy contract](https://github.com/occult-kranti/engineering-intelligence-atlas/blob/main/projects/architecture-library/site-models/ACCURACY.md)
for site identity, source provenance, scale, coverage and proposed error targets.
Use the [game art production plan](https://github.com/occult-kranti/engineering-intelligence-atlas/blob/main/projects/game-development-research/README.md)
for drawing studies, procedural art, Blender export and engine acceptance.

Hand the receiving artist/engineer a bounded packet with:

- Exact site/feature identity, epoch if known and source-owner links.
- Asset-specific license, permitted use and missing permission evidence.
- Source hash, units/coordinate uncertainty, observed coverage and gaps.
- Separately identified preserving model, preview import and game derivative.
- Capture/reconstruction/inferred-fill labels at the feature or region level.
- Intended camera distance and chosen engine/device budget.
- Actual conversion/engine evidence and the missing measurements needed for any
  higher-fidelity or metric claim.

Allow original studies inspired by forms, lighting and composition without
presenting them as measured copies of a named building. Preserve individual
temple/cave, church and mosque identity. Fine carving references need verified
registration to the whole site and compatible reuse rights before assembly.

Inspect the download and its receipt together: Blender preview imports can lose
faces even when the preserving public GLB retains them. Dense geometry, a clear
render, a video tutorial or a standard export unit does not establish survey
accuracy. Engine collision, navigation, materials and performance need separate
acceptance after import.
