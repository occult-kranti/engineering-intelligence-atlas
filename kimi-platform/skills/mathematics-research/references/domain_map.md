# Mathematics Domain Map — 19 Areas

Canonical texts, courses, and databases per area, foundational → research. Levels: F=foundational, U=undergraduate, G=graduate, R=research. Compiled 2026-11-20 from the mathematics research library; verify currency before citing editions.

## 1. Foundations & Logic
- Enderton, *A Mathematical Introduction to Logic* (U); [Open Logic Project](https://builds.openlogicproject.org/) — free modular formal logic text.
- Kunen, *Set Theory*; Jech, *Set Theory* (G–R); Nagel & Newman, *Gödel's Proof* (accessible).
- [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/) logic and foundations entries.

## 2. Arithmetic & Elementary Number Sense
- Leonardo of Pisa, *Liber Abaci* (1202/1228; English: Sigler, Springer 2002, DOI 10.1007/978-1-4613-0079-3) — the foundational European arithmetic text.
- [Khan Academy](https://www.khanacademy.org/math); [Mathigon](https://mathigon.org/); [Paul's Online Math Notes](https://tutorial.math.lamar.edu/).

## 3. Algebra (elementary → abstract)
- Gelfand, *Algebra*; Lang, *Basic Mathematics* (F).
- Artin, *Algebra* (U, preferred first abstract text); Pinter, *A Book of Abstract Algebra*; Gallian, *Contemporary Abstract Algebra*; Dummit & Foote, *Abstract Algebra* (U–G reference; a ProofNet source).
- Atiyah & Macdonald, *Introduction to Commutative Algebra*; Eisenbud, *Commutative Algebra*; Lang, *Algebra* (R).

## 4. Linear Algebra
- [MIT OCW 18.06 (Strang)](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-06-linear-algebra-spring-2010/) — full course, CC BY-NC-SA.
- Axler, *Linear Algebra Done Right* (4th ed., [open access](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-3-031-41026-0)); Treil, *Linear Algebra Done Wrong* (free PDF).
- Hoffman & Kunze; Horn & Johnson, *Matrix Analysis* (G); [3Blue1Brown Essence of Linear Algebra](https://www.3blue1brown.com/topics/linear-algebra) for intuition.

## 5. Geometry
- Euclidean: Euclid, *Elements* — [Heath translation at Clark (Joyce edition)](http://aleph0.clarku.edu/~djoyce/elements/elements.html); Hartshorne, *Geometry: Euclid and Beyond*; Stillwell, *The Four Pillars of Geometry*.
- Differential: do Carmo (U–G); Lee, *Introduction to Smooth Manifolds* and *Introduction to Riemannian Manifolds*; Spivak, 5 vols (G–R).
- Computational: de Berg et al., *Computational Geometry: Algorithms and Applications*; [CGAL](https://www.cgal.org/). Visualization: [GeoGebra](https://www.geogebra.org/), [Desmos](https://www.desmos.com/).

## 6. Calculus & Analysis
- Spivak, *Calculus*; Apostol, *Calculus*; free: [OpenStax Calculus](https://openstax.org/subjects/math), [CLP (UBC)](https://personal.math.ubc.ca/~CLP/). Courses: [MIT OCW 18.01](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-01-single-variable-calculus-fall-2006/), [18.02](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-02-multivariable-calculus-fall-2007/).
- Real analysis: Abbott → Rudin, *Principles* → Pugh; Tao, *Analysis I & II*. Multivariable: Hubbard & Hubbard; Spivak, *Calculus on Manifolds*.
- Complex: Stein & Shakarchi; Ahlfors. Graduate: Rudin, *Real and Complex Analysis*; Folland; Royden & Fitzpatrick. Formulas: [NIST DLMF](https://dlmf.nist.gov/).

## 7. Trigonometry
- [Khan Academy Trigonometry](https://www.khanacademy.org/math/trigonometry); [OpenStax Algebra and Trigonometry](https://openstax.org/details/books/algebra-and-trigonometry-2e); Gelfand & Saul, *Trigonometry*; [DLMF Ch. 4](https://dlmf.nist.gov/4).

## 8. Discrete Mathematics & Combinatorics
- [MIT OCW 6.042J Mathematics for Computer Science](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-042j-mathematics-for-computer-science-spring-2015/) (free text); Hammack, [*Book of Proof*](https://www.people.vcu.edu/~rhammack/BookOfProof/) (free); Levin, *Discrete Mathematics: An Open Introduction* (free); Rosen (reference).
- Graham, Knuth & Patashnik, *Concrete Mathematics*; Wilf, *generatingfunctionology* ([free PDF](https://www2.math.upenn.edu/~wilf/gfology2.pdf)); Bóna (U–G).
- Stanley, *Enumerative Combinatorics* Vol. 1 ([free on author's page](http://www-math.mit.edu/~rstan/ec/ec1/)); Alon & Spencer, *The Probabilistic Method*; Aigner & Ziegler, *Proofs from THE BOOK* (G–R).

## 9. Graph Theory
- Diestel, *Graph Theory* (GTM 173; [free online](https://diestel-graph-theory.com/)) — the standard; West; Bondy & Murty (U).
- Tools: [House of Graphs](https://houseofgraphs.org/) (counterexample database), [NetworkX](https://networkx.org/).
- Primary: Erdős, "Some remarks on the theory of graphs," *Bull. AMS* 53 (1947); Robertson–Sanders–Seymour–Thomas four-colour proof, *JCTB* 70 (1997), DOI 10.1006/jctb.1997.1750.

## 10. Number Theory
- Burton; Silverman, *A Friendly Introduction to Number Theory*; Dudley (F–U).
- Hardy & Wright (6th ed.); Ireland & Rosen, *A Classical Introduction to Modern Number Theory* (G).
- Primary: Gauss, *Disquisitiones Arithmeticae* (1801); Erdős–Selberg elementary PNT, *PNAS* 35 (1949).
- Tools: [PARI/GP](https://pari.math.u-bordeaux.fr/), [LMFDB](https://www.lmfdb.org/), [OEIS](https://oeis.org), Crisman's free [*Number Theory in Context*](https://math.gordon.edu/ntic/); open problems: [Erdős Problems](https://www.erdosproblems.com/).

## 11. Probability & Statistics
- [Harvard Stat 110 (Blitzstein)](https://stat110.hsites.harvard.edu/) + free book at [probabilitybook.net](http://probabilitybook.net); [MIT OCW 6.041](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-041-probabilistic-systems-analysis-and-applied-probability-fall-2010/) and [18.05](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-05-introduction-to-probability-and-statistics-spring-2022/).
- Feller Vol. 1; Ross; Pitman (U). Measure-theoretic: Williams; Billingsley; Durrett ([free v5 PDF](https://services.math.duke.edu/~rtd/PTE/PTE5_011119.pdf)); primary: Kolmogorov, *Grundbegriffe* (1933).
- Statistics: Casella & Berger; Wasserman, *All of Statistics*; McElreath, *Statistical Rethinking*. Tools: [NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/), [Seeing Theory](https://seeing-theory.brown.edu/), [R](https://www.r-project.org/). Research: Vershynin, *High-Dimensional Probability* ([free](https://www.math.uci.edu/~rvershyn/papers/HDP-book/HDP-book.html)).

## 12. Differential Equations
- Tenenbaum & Pollard; Braun; Boyce & DiPrima (U); [MIT OCW 18.03](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-03-differential-equations-spring-2010/).
- Arnold; Hirsch, Smale & Devaney; Evans, *Partial Differential Equations* (G).
- Numerical: [SciPy solve_ivp](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.integrate.solve_ivp.html), [SUNDIALS](https://computing.llnl.gov/projects/sundials), [DifferentialEquations.jl](https://docs.sciml.ai/DiffEqDocs/stable/).

## 13. Numerical Analysis
- Trefethen & Bau, *Numerical Linear Algebra* (SIAM 1997); Burden & Faires; Heath, *Scientific Computing* (U–G).
- Golub & Van Loan, *Matrix Computations*; Demmel (G–R); [MIT OCW 18.085/18.086 (Strang)](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-085-computational-science-and-engineering-i-fall-2008/).
- Tools: [Netlib](https://www.netlib.org/), [LAPACK](https://www.netlib.org/lapack/), [NumPy](https://numpy.org/)/[SciPy](https://scipy.org/), [mpmath](https://mpmath.org/).

## 14. Optimization
- Boyd & Vandenberghe, *Convex Optimization* — [free PDF](https://stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/); course [Stanford EE364a](https://www.stanford.edu/class/ee364a/).
- Nocedal & Wright, *Numerical Optimization*; Beck, *First-Order Methods*; Bubeck ([free PDF](https://arxiv.org/abs/1405.4980)); Ben-Tal & Nemirovski.
- Tools: [CVXPY](https://www.cvxpy.org/), [CVX](http://cvxr.com/cvx/), [CVXOPT](https://cvxopt.org/), [JuMP](https://jump.dev/), [HiGHS](https://highs.dev/)/[Gurobi](https://www.gurobi.com/).

## 15. Topology
- Munkres, *Topology* (the standard; a ProofNet source); free: Viro et al., *Elementary Topology: Problem Textbook*; counterexample companion: Steen & Seebach.
- Hatcher, *Algebraic Topology* ([free PDF](https://pi.math.cornell.edu/~hatcher/AT/ATpage.html)); May, *A Concise Course* ([free](https://www.math.uchicago.edu/~may/CONCISE/ConciseRevised.pdf)).
- Applied: Ghrist, *Elementary Applied Topology*; Dey & Wang, *Computational Topology for Data Analysis*.

## 16. Measure Theory
- Tao, *An Introduction to Measure Theory* ([draft on Tao's blog](https://terrytao.wordpress.com/books/an-introduction-to-measure-theory/)); Stein & Shakarchi, *Real Analysis*.
- Folland; Royden & Fitzpatrick; Rudin (G). Primary: Lebesgue's 1902 thesis; Kolmogorov 1933; Carathéodory extension.

## 17. Information Theory
- Primary: Shannon, "A Mathematical Theory of Communication," *BSTJ* 27 (1948) 379–423, 623–656; book with Weaver (1949).
- Cover & Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory* (G standard); MacKay, *Information Theory, Inference, and Learning Algorithms* ([free PDF](http://www.inference.org.uk/mackay/itila/book.html)); Yeung; El Gamal & Kim.
- [MIT OCW 6.450](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-450-principles-of-digital-communications-i-fall-2006/); Gallager.

## 18. Proofs, Problem-Solving & Mathematical Writing
- Velleman, *How to Prove It* (3rd ed.); Hammack, *Book of Proof* (free); Chartrand–Polimeni–Zhang; Houston (bridge texts).
- Pólya, *How to Solve It* (1945) and *Mathematics and Plausible Reasoning*; Tao, *Solving Mathematical Problems*.
- Knuth, Larrabee & Roberts, *Mathematical Writing* (MAA Notes 14, 1989); Krantz, *A Primer of Mathematical Writing*.

## 19. Mathematical Modeling
- [GAIMME guidelines (SIAM/COMAP 2019)](https://www.siam.org/publications/reports/guidelines-for-assessment-and-instruction-in-mathematical-modeling-education/) — modeling as a process: identify problem → assumptions/variables → do the math → analyze/assess → iterate/report.
- Practice: [COMAP MCM/ICM](https://www.comap.com/contests/mcm-icm). Texts: Meerschaert; Bender (Dover); Tung; Deisenroth et al., *Mathematics for Machine Learning* ([free](https://mml-book.github.io/)).
