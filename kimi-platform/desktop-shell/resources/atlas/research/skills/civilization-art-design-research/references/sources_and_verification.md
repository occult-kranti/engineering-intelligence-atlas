## Fail-stop policy

When no anchored source can be identified for a load-bearing tradition claim, report "unverifiable as claimed." When a pattern's provenance (site, object, accession, photograph source) cannot be established, leave it unclassified and say why. When a museum record's rights field is unreadable, exclude the asset rather than assume (route to the creative-rights-reproducibility-editor gate).
