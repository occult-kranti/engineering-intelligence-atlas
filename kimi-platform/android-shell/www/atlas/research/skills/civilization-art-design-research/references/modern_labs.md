# Modern Labs L1–L7

Every lab is a **contemporary teaching artifact**. State the modern construction method (credited to its modern author) separately from any documented historical procedure. No lab output may be captioned "reconstruction of how X was made" unless it implements a documented manual (Roriczer 1486, Topkapı Scroll plates, Robins grid) and says which.

**Lab hard gates:** pinned tool versions; license-check any code actually run (repo postures in repo_casebook.md / assets/repo_cases.json); declare random seeds; declare numeric tolerance; label outputs "modern exercise, method per [citation]".

Bundled fixtures (all original, generated in-skill, labeled modern exercises):
- `assets/exercises/roriczer_quadrature.svg` — documented-procedure exercise (L4)
- `assets/exercises/robins_grid_18square.svg`, `assets/exercises/robins_grid_amarna.svg` — canon-grid exercises (L3)
- `assets/exercises/girih_pic_decagon.svg` — modern PIC exercise (L2)
- `assets/exercises/frieze_*.svg` — one generated frieze per group (L1)
- `assets/fixtures/symmetry_known_vectors.json` — known vectors for the symmetry validator
- Regenerate all SVGs deterministically: `python3 scripts/pattern_svg_generator.py --mode all --outdir assets/exercises`

## L1. Wallpaper/frieze classification kit

- **Input evidence:** any documented border (e.g., Hoysala frieze register, Pompeian mosaic border, adinkra strip).
- **Modern method (credited):** Schattschneider (1978) recognition flowchart; Washburn–Crowe (1988) protocol.
- **Deliverable:** SVG of the motif + symmetry-group verdict with criteria statement (color handling, extension rule, census followed).
- **Fixture:** `scripts/pattern_svg_generator.py --mode frieze --group <group>` generates one SVG per frieze group (p111, p1m1, pm11, pmm2, p112, p1g1, pma2) as transformation recipes over a declared motif; `scripts/symmetry_validator.py` verifies the generated point sets are invariant under the group's declared operations within tolerance. The validator checks only declared transformations on finite fixtures — it is not a generic wallpaper-group classifier; group verdicts follow the flowchart protocol with criteria (see symmetry_and_proportion.md).
- **Pass criteria:** 10 labeled patterns, ≥9 correct with written justification; disputed cases presented as disputed.

## L2. Girih/star-pattern SVG generator

- **Input evidence:** Topkapı Scroll plates (Necipoğlu 1995); Hankin (1925).
- **Modern method (credited):** Kaplan & Salesin polygons-in-contact (PIC), *ACM ToG* 23(2) (2004): 97–119.
- **Deliverable:** parameterized SVG (contact angle, tile set); provenance note distinguishing scroll evidence from PIC inference.
- **Fixture:** `assets/exercises/girih_pic_decagon.svg` — a decagonal PIC exercise generated by `pattern_svg_generator.py --mode girih`. Its embedded note states: modern exercise, method per Kaplan & Salesin 2004; the Topkapı Scroll is the historical record; Lu & Steinhardt 2007 quasiperiodicity claims are disputed (Makovicky 2007; Cromwell 2015).
- **Pass criteria:** generated decagonal pattern matches a published PIC example tile-for-tile; provenance/dispute note present.

## L3. Proportion-canon figure lab

- **Input evidence:** Robins (1994) grid tables.
- **Modern method (credited):** grid-overlay drawing exercise on PD/CC0 New Kingdom and Amarna images (Met Open Access, CC0 — per-record rights field controls).
- **Deliverable:** two drawn figures (18-square canon; Amarna canon) + a short note on what the grid does NOT prove.
- **Fixture:** `assets/exercises/robins_grid_18square.svg` and `robins_grid_amarna.svg` — original schematic figures on 18- and 21-square grids with Amarna proportions, generated by `pattern_svg_generator.py --mode robins-grid`. The schematic figure is the lab author's; it is not a traced artifact.
- **Pass criteria:** correct square counts; note distinguishes documented canon from "ideal beauty" claims.

## L4. Roriczer pinnacle exercise (documented quadrature)

- **Input evidence:** Roriczer 1486 (*Büchlein von der Fialen Gerechtigkeit*, Regensburg); facsimile/transcription in Shelby (1977).
- **Method:** literal compass-and-straightedge ad quadratum construction (rotation of squares), then optional CAD replication (OpenSCAD GPL-2.0+CGAL exception; FreeCAD LGPL-2.1 — see repo cases).
- **Deliverable:** stepwise SVG (+ optional CAD sketch); each step cross-referenced to the 1486 text.
- **Fixture:** `assets/exercises/roriczer_quadrature.svg` — the rotation-of-squares sequence (outer square → 45°-rotated inscribed square → iteration) implementing the documented Roriczer procedure, generated by `pattern_svg_generator.py --mode quadrature`. Steps are annotated with the manual's operation; exact closure is exact by construction (vertices computed geometrically, not measured).
- **Pass criteria:** construction closes (final point within declared tolerance) and every step cites a sentence of the manual.

## L5. Stepwell transformation study

- **Input evidence:** Chand Baori plan/photos (ASI; Livingston 2002). Dating c. 8th–9th c., debated — no dating claim beyond the cited range.
- **Modern method (credited):** reflection/glide-array analysis + isometric CAD exercise.
- **Deliverable:** annotated axonometric naming the transformation group of the step array.
- **Pass criteria:** group verdict with criteria statement; no dating claim beyond the cited range.

## L6. Museum open-access provenance lab

- **Input evidence:** Met Open Access CSV + Smithsonian Open Access (both CC0 — repo cases).
- **Method:** IIIF Image/Presentation API manifests (cite spec by name + version).
- **Deliverable:** small IIIF manifest + provenance card per object (accession number, credit line, license).
- **Pass criteria:** manifest validates; every image carries the rights statement from the source record, not assumed; umbrella CC0 never substitutes for the per-record rights field (creative-rights gate).

## L7. Pigment/color palette study

- **Input evidence:** Gettens & Stout (1942); Berke 2007; Cennini/Theophilus recipes.
- **Method:** recreate a documented palette as digital swatches with chemistry notes (no physical pigment handling without lab safety review).
- **Deliverable:** swatch sheet with pigment name, chemistry, period/regional attestation, toxicity flag.
- **Pass criteria:** each swatch cites attestation region/period; no "universal medieval palette."
