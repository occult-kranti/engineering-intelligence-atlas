## Measures

- Task success (per criterion), time-on-task, error counts.
- SEQ after each task (1–7).
- SUS (Brooke 1996) at END of session only — score ×2.5 → 0–100, report vs.
  the 68-norm percentile; never as a percentage. (Or UMUX-LITE per Lewis,
  Utesch & Maher 2013.)
- Accessibility inclusion: <which participants use AT; a study without AT users
  supports zero accessibility claims>.

## Analysis plan

Raw evidence (notes/recordings, consent-recorded) kept separate from
interpretation. Findings ranked by evidence tier (observed behavior >
self-report > heuristic judgment) with n, segment, and limits stated. The
synthesis includes a "what this evidence does NOT support" section.
