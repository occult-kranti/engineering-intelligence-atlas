# Biology Domain Map — 16 Disciplines

Compiled 2026-11-20. Access notes: free/open unless marked paid or registered. Verify editions and database releases before citing.

## 1. Molecular & Cell Biology
- Alberts et al., *Molecular Biology of the Cell* (7th ed., 2022; paid; searchable 4th ed. free on [NCBI Bookshelf](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK21054/)); Alberts, *Essential Cell Biology*; Lodish et al., *Molecular Cell Biology*; Weinberg, *The Biology of Cancer*.
- Milo & Phillips, *Cell Biology by the Numbers* — free at [book.bionumbers.org](http://book.bionumbers.org/), pairs with [BioNumbers](https://bionumbers.hms.harvard.edu/).
- Courses: MITx [7.00x Secret of Life](https://web.mit.edu/mitxbio/courses.html), [7.06x Cell Biology](https://web.mit.edu/mitxbio/courses.html), [MIT OCW 7.012](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/7-012-introduction-to-biology-fall-2004/); [HHMI BioInteractive](https://www.biointeractive.org/); [iBiology](https://www.ibiology.org/).
- Reviews: *Nature Reviews Molecular Cell Biology*, *Annual Review of Cell and Developmental Biology*.

## 2. Genetics & Genomics
- Courses: [MIT 7.03x Genetics](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/res-7-006-7-03x-genetics/), [7.28x Molecular Biology](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/res-7-008-7-28x-molecular-biology/), [HST.508 Genomics and Computational Biology](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/hst-508-genomics-and-computational-biology-fall-2002/). Texts: Griffiths; Klug & Cummings (paid).
- Databases (free): [NCBI](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/) (GenBank, RefSeq, Gene, dbSNP, ClinVar, PubMed, Taxonomy, BLAST), [Ensembl](https://www.ensembl.org/), [UCSC Genome Browser](https://genome.ucsc.edu/) (academic), [gnomAD](https://gnomad.broadinstitute.org/), [ClinVar](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/clinvar/), [GTEx](https://gtexportal.org/), [1000 Genomes/IGSR](https://www.internationalgenome.org/), [OMIM](https://omim.org/) (registered), [UK Biobank](https://www.ukbiobank.ac.uk/) (registered, fees).

## 3. Proteins & Structural Biology
- [UniProt](https://www.uniprot.org/) (UniProtKB Swiss-Prot/TrEMBL, UniRef, UniParc; CC BY 4.0); [RCSB PDB](https://www.rcsb.org/)/[PDBe](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pdbe/) (experimental structures, CC0).
- [AlphaFold Protein Structure Database](https://alphafold.ebi.ac.uk/) — 200M+ predicted structures. **Caveats:** predictions, not experimental structures; interpret pLDDT/PAE; record model version (v6 as of 2026); known sync lag vs. UniProt releases ([AlphaSync](https://alphasync.stjude.org) addresses currency). AlphaFold 3 server: free for non-commercial use.
- Annotation: [InterPro](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/interpro/), Pfam, CDD, STRING, Reactome, KEGG (web free, FTP paid).

## 4. Evolution & Phylogenetics
- [UC Berkeley Understanding Evolution](https://evolution.berkeley.edu/); Futuyma & Kirkpatrick; Freeman & Herron (paid); Gillespie, *Population Genetics*; Coop's free [popgen notes](https://github.com/cooplab/popgen-notes).
- Data/tools: [Open Tree of Life](https://opentreeoflife.org/), [TimeTree](http://timetree.org/), [Tree of Life Web Project](http://tolweb.org/tree/); software: IQ-TREE, RAxML-NG, MrBayes, BEAST 2, MEGA, MAFFT/MUSCLE; [PHYLIP list](http://evolution.genetics.washington.edu/phylip/software.html).

## 5. Ecology & Biodiversity Data
- OpenStax [*Biology 2e*](https://openstax.org/details/books/biology-2e) and [*Concepts of Biology*](https://openstax.org/details/books/concepts-biology) (CC BY); Begon et al., *Ecology: From Individuals to Ecosystems* (paid).
- Infrastructure: [GBIF](https://www.gbif.org/) (cite downloads via DOI), [IUCN Red List](https://www.iucnredlist.org/) (non-commercial, API token), [iNaturalist](https://www.inaturalist.org/)/[eBird](https://ebird.org/), [BOLD](https://www.boldsystems.org/) (barcodes).

## 6. Physiology
- OpenStax [*Anatomy and Physiology 2e*](https://openstax.org/details/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e) (CC BY); Boron & Boulpaep; Guyton & Hall (paid, clinical depth); [PhysioNet](https://physionet.org/) (mixed licenses, credentialed clinical datasets); Taiz et al., *Plant Physiology and Development*.

## 7. Microbiology
- OpenStax [*Microbiology*](https://openstax.org/details/books/microbiology) (CC BY); Baron et al., [*Medical Microbiology* 4th ed.](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK7627/) (free, dated clinically); Brock; Prescott's (paid).
- Databases: [BacDive](https://bacdive.dsmz.de/), [LPSN](https://lpsn.dsmz.de/), [GTDB](https://gtdb.ecogenomic.org/), [IMG/M](https://img.jgi.doe.gov/); [VFDB](http://www.mgc.ac.cn/VFs/) and [PHI-base](http://www.phi-base.org/) — use strictly for understanding pathogenesis/defense, never engineering (see safety.md).
- Microbiome analysis: QIIME 2, DADA2, phyloseq; [Orchestrating Microbiome Analysis with Bioconductor](https://bioconductor.org/books/release/OMA/).

## 8. Immunology
- Janeway et al., [*Immunobiology* 5th ed.](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK10757/) (free, concepts classic); current: Murphy & Weaver (paid); Abbas; Sompayrac.
- Data: [ImmPort](https://www.immport.org/) (registration), [IEDB](https://www.iedb.org/), [VDJdb](https://vdjdb.cdr3.net/), [IMGT](https://www.imgt.org/) (academic).

## 9. Neuroscience
- Kandel et al., *Principles of Neural Science* (6th ed., 2025; paid); Purves et al., *Neuroscience* (1st ed. free on [NCBI Bookshelf](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK10799/)); OpenStax [*Psychology 2e*](https://openstax.org/details/books/psychology-2e).
- Data/tools: [Allen Brain Atlas](https://portal.brain-map.org/), [OpenNeuro](https://openneuro.org/) (CC0), [NeuroMorpho.Org](https://neuromorpho.org/), [Neuromatch Academy](https://neuromatch.io/academy/), EBRAINS (registration); MIT OCW 9.x.

## 10. Developmental Biology
- Gilbert & Barresi, *Developmental Biology*; Wolpert et al., *Principles of Development* (paid).
- Model-organism databases (free): [WormBase](https://wormbase.org/), [FlyBase](https://flybase.org/), [ZFIN](https://zfin.org/), [MGI](http://www.informatics.jax.org/), [Xenbase](https://www.xenbase.org/), [TAIR](https://www.arabidopsis.org/) (partly subscription).
- Frontier venues: [eLife](https://elifesciences.org/), *Development*.

## 11. Bioinformatics, Computational & Systems Biology
- Learning: Durbin et al., *Biological Sequence Analysis*; [Rosalind](http://rosalind.info/); [Bioinformatics.ca workshops](https://bioinformatics.ca/workshops/); [EMBL-EBI Training](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/training/); [Bioconductor courses](https://www.bioconductor.org/help/course-materials/).
- Software (open source unless noted): alignment — BLAST+, DIAMOND, MMseqs2, minimap2, Bowtie2, BWA-MEM2, MAFFT, MUSCLE5, Clustal Omega; NGS — FastQC/MultiQC, fastp, samtools/bcftools, GATK (academic), DeepVariant, STAR, Salmon/kallisto, DESeq2/edgeR/limma, Seurat/Scanpy; workflows — [Nextflow](https://www.nextflow.io/) + [nf-core](https://nf-co.re/), Snakemake, WDL/Cromwell, [Galaxy](https://usegalaxy.org/), Bioconductor, Biopython; structure/chem — PyMOL (open build), ChimeraX (academic), Foldseek, RDKit; systems — SBML, [BioModels](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/biomodels/), COPASI, Tellurium, [Cytoscape](https://cytoscape.org/).
- AI era (2025–2026): AlphaFold 3 (non-commercial server), ESM-3 (license-gated weights), Evo 2 (genome modeling), scFoundation/Geneformer (single-cell). Agent-facing APIs: UniProt/Ensembl/GBIF/Open Tree REST; `gget`. Treat all generative-design outputs as hypotheses requiring screening and validation (safety.md).

## 12. Synthetic Biology
- [iGEM Registry](https://parts.igem.org/) and [competition/safety hub](https://igem.org/); [Addgene](https://www.addgene.org/synthetic-biology/) (educational content free; physical plasmids via institutional MTA); [SynBioHub](https://synbiohub.org/) + [SBOL](https://sbolstandard.org/); [SEVA plasmids](https://seva-plasmids.com/).
- Texts: Freemont & Kitney; Kuldell et al., *BioBuilder* (open access).
- **Boundary:** discuss standards, modeling, and literature — never output construct-level build instructions for regulated or hazardous agents (safety.md).

## 13. Taxonomy & Systematics
- [Catalogue of Life](https://www.catalogueoflife.org/) (CC BY, DOI-citable annual releases), [NCBI Taxonomy](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/taxonomy), [GBIF Backbone](https://www.gbif.org/dataset/d7dddbf4-2cf0-4f39-9b2a-bb099caae36c), [ITIS](https://www.itis.gov/), [WoRMS](https://www.marinespecies.org/), [Index Fungorum](http://www.indexfungorum.org/), [IPNI](https://www.ipni.org/), [IRMNG](https://www.irmng.org/).
- Name resolution: [Global Names Verifier](https://verifier.globalnames.org/data_sources), Open Tree TNRS. Nomenclatural codes: ICN (plants), ICZN (animals), ICNP/SeqCode (prokaryotes); follow IJSEM validation lists.

## 14. Statistics for Biology
- Holmes & Huber, [*Modern Statistics for Modern Biology*](https://www.huber.embl.de/msmb/) — free, R/Bioconductor-based; top pick.
- Wickham & Grolemund, [*R for Data Science*](https://r4ds.hadley.nz/); James et al., [*An Introduction to Statistical Learning*](https://www.statlearning.com/); Hastie et al., *Elements of Statistical Learning* (free PDFs); Harrell, [*Biostatistics for Biomedical Research*](https://hbiostat.org/bbr/).
- [OpenIntro Statistics](https://www.openintro.org/book/os/), [Seeing Theory](https://seeing-theory.brown.edu/), [StatQuest](https://statquest.org/), Nature Methods [*Points of Significance*](https://www.nature.com/collections/qghhqm), [`vegan`](https://cran.r-project.org/package=vegan).
- Inference reform: preregistration, Registered Reports, multiverse analysis, equivalence testing, Bayesian workflow.

## 15. Reproducible & Open Research
- FAIR: [Wilkinson et al. 2016](https://www.nature.com/articles/sdata201618) + [GO FAIR](https://www.go-fair.org/fair-principles/). Preregistration: [OSF](https://osf.io/), [COS Registered Reports](https://www.cos.io/initiatives/registered-reports), [PCI RR](https://rr.peercommunityin.org/).
- Preprints: [bioRxiv](https://www.biorxiv.org/), [medRxiv](https://www.medrxiv.org/), [EcoEvoRxiv](https://ecoevorxiv.org/), [PCI](https://peercommunityin.org/).
- Repositories: [Zenodo](https://zenodo.org/), [Dryad](https://datadryad.org/), [Figshare](https://figshare.com/), [GEO](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/)/[SRA](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/sra)/[ENA](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/ena)/[BioStudies](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/biostudies/), GitHub + [Software Heritage](https://www.softwareheritage.org/).
- Environments: Docker/Singularity-Apptainer, Conda/Bioconda, renv; nf-core CI testing. Standards: MIAME/MINSEQE/MIAS checklists; [EQUATOR](https://www.equator-network.org/) reporting guidelines; PRISMA 2020 / CONSORT 2025.
- AI-data caveat: computationally generated data needs documented methods and quantifiable confidence before reuse.

## 16. Primary Literature Access
- [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/)/[Europe PMC](https://europepmc.org/) (OA full text via [PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/)); [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/), [Semantic Scholar](https://www.semanticscholar.org/) (free API), [OpenAlex](https://openalex.org/) (CC0), [CORE](https://core.ac.uk/), [Unpaywall](https://unpaywall.org/) (legal OA copies).
- [Lens.org](https://www.lens.org/), [Scite](https://scite.ai/) (paid), [Connected Papers](https://www.connectedpapers.com/)/[Research Rabbit](https://www.researchrabbit.ai/).
- Strong OA venues: eLife, PLOS family, BMC, Frontiers, *Nucleic Acids Research* annual database issue.
- **Never** route users to pirate mirrors; note paywall status and legal OA alternatives.
