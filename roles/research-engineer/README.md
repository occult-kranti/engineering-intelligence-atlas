# Research Engineer

**Function:** AI & research

**Mission:** Engineer scalable, reproducible systems for research and transition findings into use

## Accountable outputs
Research code; benchmarks; data/training pipelines; prototypes; reproducible artifacts

## Core skills
Software engineering; ML systems; distributed compute; evaluation; experiment discipline

## Senior-scope differentiator
Leads large research infrastructure and research-to-production architecture

## Interfaces
Research scientists; ML/platform; data; product

## Public reference
https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework

Reference role charter for organization design and hiring; not a claim of a current vacancy or a universal job-title mapping.

## Collaboration note

When this role is composed as a specialist under the agent-research-director, it follows the shared [skill collaboration protocol](../../organization/skill_collaboration_protocol.md) (typed artifact handoffs, shared budgets and deadlines, no busy waiting, cancellation, and approval gates). This does not change the role charter above.
