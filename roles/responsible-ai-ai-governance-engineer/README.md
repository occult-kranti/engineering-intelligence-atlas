**Function:** Responsible technology

**Mission:** Translate AI policy and risk into technical controls, evidence, and operating practice

AI inventory; risk assessments; control libraries; review evidence; incident/decommission workflow

## Core skills
AI lifecycle; policy; evaluation; privacy/security; documentation; stakeholder facilitation

## Senior-scope differentiator
Shapes enterprise AI governance and assures independence of challenge

## Interfaces
AI teams; legal; privacy; security; product; audit
