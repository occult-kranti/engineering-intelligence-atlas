# Director of Engineering

**Function:** Leadership & governance

**Mission:** Build a multi-team engineering area with predictable outcomes and durable capability

## Accountable outputs
Area roadmap; org/capacity design; delivery and operational health; leadership bench

## Core skills
Multi-team leadership; planning; technical literacy; cross-functional negotiation

## Senior-scope differentiator
Shapes several teams and develops managers/staff ICs

## Interfaces
VP; EMs; product/design directors; TPM; security

## Public reference
https://sfia-online.org/en/sfia-9

Reference role charter for organization design and hiring; not a claim of a current vacancy or a universal job-title mapping.
