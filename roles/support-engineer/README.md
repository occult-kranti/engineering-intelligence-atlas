# Support Engineer

**Function:** Support & knowledge

**Mission:** Resolve complex customer technical issues and feed reproducible signals into engineering

## Accountable outputs
Reproductions; escalations; KB articles; defect trends; customer communication

## Core skills
Debugging; logs/traces; product/domain knowledge; communication; scripting

## Senior-scope differentiator
Builds diagnostic tooling and systemic product improvements

## Interfaces
Engineering; product; docs; SRE; customer success

## Public reference
https://sfia-online.org/en/sfia-9

Reference role charter for organization design and hiring; not a claim of a current vacancy or a universal job-title mapping.
