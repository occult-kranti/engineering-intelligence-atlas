# Legal / Policy / Compliance Specialist

**Function:** Legal, trust & policy

**Mission:** Identify and operationalize applicable legal, regulatory, contractual, and policy obligations

## Accountable outputs
Requirement/risk register; review guidance; contract/policy evidence; escalation; compliance monitoring

## Core skills
Applicable legal/domain knowledge; research; risk; writing; cross-functional judgment

## Senior-scope differentiator
Owns organization-level policy strategy and independent challenge within professional scope

## Interfaces
Leadership; engineering; security; privacy; marketing; people

## Public reference
https://www.acm.org/code-of-ethics

Reference role charter for organization design and hiring; not a claim of a current vacancy or a universal job-title mapping.

When this role is composed as a specialist under the agent-research-director, it follows the shared [skill collaboration protocol](../../organization/skill_collaboration_protocol.md) (typed artifact handoffs, shared budgets and deadlines, no busy waiting, cancellation, and approval gates).
