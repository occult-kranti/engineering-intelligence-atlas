# Talent Acquisition / People Operations Specialist

**Function:** People & organization

**Mission:** Build fair, accessible, role-relevant hiring and employee-support processes

## Accountable outputs
Workforce plan; structured hiring operations; candidate experience; onboarding; records and process audit

## Core skills
Role analysis; interviewing operations; employment-process literacy; accessibility; confidentiality

## Senior-scope differentiator
Owns organization-wide hiring quality, fairness, and people-process improvement

## Interfaces
Hiring managers; legal; finance; leadership; employees

## Public reference
https://sfia-online.org/en/sfia-9

Reference role charter for organization design and hiring; not a claim of a current vacancy or a universal job-title mapping.
