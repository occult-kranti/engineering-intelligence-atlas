# Behavioral / Research Psychologist

**Function:** Research & responsible growth

**Mission:** Study human behavior, cognition, motivation, learning, and wellbeing with valid research methods

## Accountable outputs
Research questions; ethical study design; validated measures; analysis; limitations and harm review

## Core skills
Psychological science; statistics; research ethics; experimental design; communication

## Senior-scope differentiator
Leads rigorous behavioral research and challenges unsupported psychological claims

## Interfaces
Product; UX research; data; legal; community; leadership

## Public reference
https://www.acm.org/code-of-ethics

Reference role charter for organization design and hiring; not a claim of a current vacancy or a universal job-title mapping.
