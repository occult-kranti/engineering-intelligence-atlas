# Agent Research Director / Orchestration Lead

**Function:** Agent governance & research

**Mission:** Coordinate specialist agents and evidence to produce auditable research and engineering outcomes

## Accountable outputs
Research plan; delegation contracts; source/evidence matrix; synthesis; conflict/termination log; evaluation

## Core skills
Research methods; agent orchestration; evaluation; risk/security; technical writing; leadership

## Senior-scope differentiator
Owns multi-domain research quality and controlled agent-system strategy

## Interfaces
Domain leads; security; engineering; product; legal; users

## Public reference
https://agentskills.io/specification

Reference role charter for organization design and hiring; not a claim of a current vacancy or a universal job-title mapping.

## Collaboration note

When this role is composed as a specialist under the agent-research-director, it follows the shared [skill collaboration protocol](../../organization/skill_collaboration_protocol.md) (typed artifact handoffs, shared budgets and deadlines, no busy waiting, cancellation, and approval gates).
