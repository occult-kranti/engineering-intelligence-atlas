# Market Research / Competitive Intelligence Analyst

**Function:** Market & customer research

**Mission:** Produce sourced, unbiased evidence about customers, competitors, markets, and adoption constraints

## Accountable outputs
Research plan; source/evidence matrix; market analysis; competitor comparisons; uncertainty report

## Core skills
Research methods; statistics; industry analysis; citation discipline; writing; ethics

## Senior-scope differentiator
Owns strategic market-learning programs and challenges weak assumptions

## Interfaces
Product; leadership; marketing; sales; data; legal

## Public reference
https://sfia-online.org/en/sfia-9
