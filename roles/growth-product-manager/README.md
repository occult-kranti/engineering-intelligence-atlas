# Growth Product Manager

**Function:** Marketing & growth

**Mission:** Improve activation, adoption, retention, and sustainable reach through ethical product experiments

## Accountable outputs
Growth model; experiment backlog; funnel evidence; lifecycle analysis; learning repository

## Core skills
Product management; statistics; UX/research; analytics; experimentation; economics

## Senior-scope differentiator
Owns portfolio-level growth learning and long-term trust/quality tradeoffs

## Interfaces
Product; design; data; engineering; marketing; support
