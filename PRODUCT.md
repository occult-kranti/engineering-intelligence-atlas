# Daftar — the connected company office

## Product outcome

Turn repositories into companies on one continuous blueprint plane. Company boundaries, offices, departments, flat employee circles, Donna conversation and work controls occupy the same spatial world. The latest laptop direction removes the physical conference-table underlay. Choosing a company or department moves the focus within that world. Donna coordinates saved assistants and shared specialists through goals, scoped authority, queues, budgets, evidence and handoffs. Company parent relationships and departments within departments organize the portfolio without transferring ownership or permissions.

## Audiences and surfaces

- The owner uses the Freeflow blueprint world, company offices, roadmap, people, model pool and ledger as elements of the same canvas in the desktop or private website. Panels can be found, moved, resized, pinned, collapsed and reopened without replacing their underlying forms.
- Signed-in website owners enter the canvas by default. Connections, access and applications open within it. Standalone sign-in, invitation and visiting-employee entry flows retain their own authentication and restricted-data behavior.
- Visiting employees receive an explicit company role and expiry. Their canvas, notes and proposals use a restricted server projection; unavailable areas remain greyed out without exposing hidden content.
- The headless service runs the Rust core and worker pool on a persistent Linux host. Website and MCP clients control bounded work while jobs remain owned by that runtime.
- The public atlas and starter preview contain only public research and synthetic example offices.

## Product truth

Native state is the execution authority. Saved employee definitions are not running processes. Every dispatched provider request has a bounded budget, a current scope and a durable receipt. Shared service contracts and knowledge grants permit explicit cross-company handoffs; a parent company relation never creates an authority grant. Subdepartment nesting is organizational and does not cascade lifecycle changes.

The Donna crew projection resolves the actual Donna, company/office assistants and department coordinators from saved identities. Shared specialists require matching service contracts and scope. Missing, duplicate or mismatched definitions stay unconfigured. The crew can propose a scoped draft goal or pause/resume action; native authority validates the submitted command. Drafting a goal does not dispatch a provider request, while resuming an already authorized queue can allow work to start.

The permanent observer checks index coverage, ledger identities, recorded costs and live job leases without model requests. It reports issues rather than inventing repairs. The native index refreshes with saved commands. Shared local model weights and download receipts are host resources; company prompts, artifacts, assignments and job budgets keep their own boundaries.

Scoped [continuous improvement](design/continuous-improvement/README.md) adds persistent producer/helper cycles under one immutable parent allowance of at most USD 20. Every cycle reserves both phases and requires exact-artifact review before advancing. Colored comment/todo notes attach to native office/department scope. Activity lighting uses fresh runtime callback evidence; saved employee definitions and loaded model inventory remain separate from executing work. The owner website waits for the native mutation receipt before acknowledging an improvement edit.

Source Review is another deterministic runtime service under Donna. It classifies external text already read by the managed GitHub snapshot path or prepared for an OfficeWorker request, records coverage and findings, and hands flagged material to Donna. Source documents cannot grant tools, change authority or override the owner's task. Robot exclusion preferences are classified separately; an exact-source advisory override is a policy decision, not a website visit. There is no general website crawler in this increment.

The endless canvas is a presentation system with bounded geometry, panel counts and scene rendering. It preserves scope, forms and drafts; it does not create an unlimited database, bypass membership checks or start workers. Stored layout contains panel geometry and presentation preferences, not form values, credentials or access grants. Restricted member projections remain restricted before rendering.

The desktop encrypts provider credentials in the OS vault. A VPS uses private operator-managed secret files. Worker startup is paused. Real model downloads, infrastructure rentals, account connections and installed-device acceptance require actual configured resources. The authorized development Claude ceiling remains USD 100, separate from individual job allowances.

The Kimi branch is an additive historical product archive. Selected coordination and interaction ideas inform current implementations; its old runtime and binaries do not replace the native authority. [The branch review](project/KIMI_BRANCH_REVIEW.md) records exact provenance and adoption boundaries.

## Release acceptance

1. Desktop, browser, public preview and restricted visitor paths resolve their complete renderer asset graph, and the owner entry route reaches the continuous canvas without a sign-in loop.
2. Canvas movement, focus, persistence, reduced motion and keyboard controls preserve usable forms and drafts. Large or malformed saved layouts cannot hide every route back to a surface.
3. Every company/department relationship is acyclic and validated by the native core; replay and concurrent changes preserve state.
4. Membership revocation, expiry and owner deactivation are checked at the operation boundary. Crew and review projections reveal only the permitted scope.
5. Native, provider and publication evidence is reported separately from mocked or synthetic checks. Historical Kimi counts do not certify the current release.
6. Source, deployment recipes, release notes and validation receipts are published together after required gates pass. [The 0.8.0 receipt](project/ENDLESS_CANVAS_VALIDATION.md) tracks this candidate separately from published 0.7.0 evidence.
