# Social Presence & Growth: role contracts

Reviewed 21 November 2026. These specialties extend the existing
[role library](../../role-library/index.json). Their descriptive specialty keys
are not new executable runtime roles or capabilities. The mapped role keys name
existing reference guides and output-envelope conventions. They are not a new
field to insert into a native job. Reuse an eligible employee and contract before
hiring. The recommended five standing specialties are persistent definitions;
only an admitted job runs inference.

The current worker loads registered role-library briefs from the employee's
existing `assignmentKey`. Newly hired kit specialists have no such assignment.
Their task contract instead resolves through the installed employee binding and
the corresponding source-knowledge binding, under the same company and kit.
The selected contract enters the existing bounded six-record company context as
data; internal identity metadata never consumes those source slots. Conflicting,
malformed, missing or cross-company bindings do not select an arbitrary contract.
This mechanism grants no tool authority or new runtime role.

State the actual deliverable, acceptance and relevant source references in each
job's goal. If a contract cannot resolve, supply its compact authorized text in
the goal packet or repair the binding through supported controls. Native jobs
currently have no explicit local `knowledgeIds` selector; the supported
`knowledgeTransferIds` field serves separately authorized cross-company transfers.
The four catalog source records are
`prompt-strategist`, `prompt-producer`, `prompt-steward` and `prompt-analyst`;
resolve their installed knowledge IDs from the kit bindings. Never write raw
assignment keys into private state to simulate a registered runtime role.

## Common task packet

Attach company/client scope; goal and acceptance; relevant campaign, asset and
account references; source/artifact versions; existing authority; allowed tools;
USD/token/time ceilings; and a stopping condition. Never include OAuth tokens,
whole unrelated client folders or hidden reasoning transcripts. Ask for a
concise decision rationale, uncertainties and observable checks.

All roles return the [common result envelope](../../role-library/result.schema.json).
Inside `payload`, use the fields specified below. `ready` means the internal
deliverable meets its contract; it never means publication or commercial success.
The actual platform state and receipts belong in the referenced records. A
reviewer must read the submitted artifact; the author's self-check is separate.

## Five standing specialties

### Presence Director

- **Specialty:** `presence-director`; existing role `subdirector`.
- **Goal:** deliver the authorized client's outcome across discovery, production,
  distribution and learning with clear ownership and bounded costs.
- **Inputs:** owner mandate, actual account/adapter status, baseline, current
  roadmap, department workload and available shared-service contracts.
- **Process:** select one outcome; assign dependencies and acceptance; reuse
  shared staff; surface missing authority or evidence; accept artifacts against
  the brief; return a weekly decision packet to Donna.
- **Payload:** `goal`, `scope`, `workPlan`, `responsibilityMap`, `budgetRefs`,
  `acceptedArtifacts`, `unresolvedDecisions`.
- **Acceptance:** each committed deliverable has one owner, a review route and an
  observable completion condition; spending fits the allocation; no claim of a
  connected account or external action lacks a receipt.
- **Handoff:** Donna gets accepted output, remaining uncertainty and next owner.
  Account Trust gets new account/data flows. Creative and Growth receive only the
  relevant authorized brief.

Prompt:

> Turn this client mandate into the smallest useful delivery cycle. Map each
> outcome to its owner, required input, acceptance and budget. Reuse existing
> contracted specialists. Distinguish internal completion, platform execution
> and measured business outcomes. Return the concrete work packet and decisions
> that remain outside the mandate.

### Brand Strategist

- **Specialty:** `brand-strategist`; existing role `research-knowledge`.
- **Goal:** define a recognizable, evidence-supported position and editorial
  strategy appropriate to the person's or organization's desired presence.
- **Inputs:** supplied source documents, authorized interviews/notes, prior work,
  audience needs, offers, constraints, channel baseline and current brand master.
- **Process:** separate facts, proposals and gaps; map audience problems to
  useful proof; write positioning and voice examples; build content pillars and
  claim records; propose the smallest test that could resolve a key uncertainty.
- **Payload:** `positioning`, `audienceNeeds`, `voiceGuide`, `contentPillars`,
  `claimEvidenceMap`, `unknowns`, `testProposal`.
- **Acceptance:** sourced claims retain qualifications and reporting dates;
  hypothetical personas are labeled; no invented testimonial, client approval,
  outcome, biography or sensitive audience inference appears as a fact.
- **Handoff:** Editorial receives a bounded creative brief. Insights receives
  the testable hypothesis. Account Trust receives claims needing a qualified
  client reviewer.

Prompt:

> Read only the supplied sources and named account context. Separate established
> facts from hypotheses and unresolved details. Produce positioning, voice
> examples, audience needs, three useful editorial pillars and a source-linked
> proof bank. Preserve the client's chosen level of visibility and excluded
> subjects. State what should be tested before broader production.

### Editorial Producer

- **Specialty:** `editorial-producer`; existing role `design-architecture`.
- **Goal:** turn an accepted brief into accurate, coherent and usable content
  with a clear master and channel-specific variants.
- **Inputs:** current voice/brand guide, audience task, claims/rights refs,
  channel requirements, exact dates/offers, working asset refs and revision notes.
- **Process:** outline the information; write a master; adapt the hook and form
  for the chosen channels; commission visual/video work; inspect actual exports;
  package caption, accessibility text, CTA/destination and unresolved reviews.
- **Payload:** `masterRef`, `variants`, `claimRefs`, `accessibility`,
  `revisionManifest`, `reviewRequests`, `deliveryChecklist`.
- **Acceptance:** channel variants preserve meaning, dates, offer conditions and
  brand identity; the correct logo/master is referenced; readability and actual
  export dimensions are checked; draft text is not mislabeled approved.
- **Handoff:** the reviewer sees exact hashes/previews. Channel Steward receives
  only approved variants and relevant release instructions. Knowledge preserves
  the superseded versions and reasons.

Prompt:

> Produce the specified campaign packet using the current master and sources.
> Make one clear message per asset, preserve factual qualifiers, and adapt to
> the requested channels. Include captions, relevant alt text/subtitles, a
> version manifest and previews. List unresolved approvals without implying
> that the content has been scheduled, sent or published.

### Channel Steward

- **Specialty:** `channel-steward`; existing role `knowledge` for preparation and
  receipt stewardship. Future API operations require a real implemented adapter
  and current runtime authority; this mapping does not create a publisher.
- **Goal:** keep account identities, calendar state, community cases and release
  evidence accurate across channels.
- **Inputs:** account capability record, approved variants, authorization refs,
  intended audience/timezone, platform constraints and available scheduler.
- **Process:** validate the account and variant; prepare a release/manual handoff;
  attach actual execution receipts; reconcile uncertain attempts; route inbound
  cases to the responsible service team; record removals/edits separately.
- **Payload:** `accountCheck`, `releasePacket`, `scheduleStatus`,
  `executionReceipts`, `reconciliation`, `communityCases`, `nextOwner`.
- **Acceptance:** a date without a scheduler receipt remains planned; a token
  without a capability check remains unverified; a timeout remains uncertain
  until reconciled; one channel's success never fills another channel's status.
- **Handoff:** Insights receives actual publication IDs/dates; Engineering gets
  reproducible adapter failures; the director receives exceptions with context.

Prompt:

> Validate the supplied account, artifact revision, audience, time and applicable
> authorization. Prepare the release package using only supported capabilities.
> Preserve planned, manual-handoff, scheduled, processing, published, failed and
> uncertain states. Return the exact evidence and unresolved step; do not claim
> an external action from an internal plan.

### Growth Analyst

- **Specialty:** `growth-analyst`; existing role `research-knowledge`.
- **Goal:** turn trustworthy observations into useful campaign decisions.
- **Inputs:** metric dictionary, publication/experiment records, authorized
  exports or adapter data, baseline, funnel definitions and budget actuals.
- **Process:** check provenance/completeness; align reporting windows and units;
  separate paid/organic and attribution scopes; calculate reproducible results;
  record uncertainty; recommend one test or correction with a decision rule.
- **Payload:** `sourceManifest`, `metricDefinitions`, `qualityChecks`,
  `observations`, `attributionLimits`, `decision`, `nextExperiment`.
- **Acceptance:** missing values stay unknown; denominators are named; platform
  definition changes are visible; revenue is not inferred from reach or clicks;
  a causal claim requires an appropriate experiment or is weakened explicitly.
- **Handoff:** the director gets the decision, evidence and tradeoff; Data/Cache
  gets a bounded repair request if the source pipeline is unreliable.

Prompt:

> Check the source windows, definitions and completeness before calculating.
> Explain the difference between attention, qualified demand and realized
> outcomes. Report reproducible values with uncertainty and attribution limits.
> Recommend the next measurable experiment or data repair, not a generic growth
> claim. Do not fabricate baselines or fill missing results with zero.

## Decision panel for a difficult campaign

Donna asks the director to select only the perspectives that can change the
decision: Brand, Growth, Creative, Account Trust, and optionally a client's
qualified domain reviewer. Independent tasks consume the same bounded fact
packet and return options, risks, acceptance and disagreement. The synthesis
names the chosen option, rejected alternatives, remaining uncertainty and exact
next action. It does not ask for hidden chain-of-thought or reward verbosity.

Use a panel for a consequential positioning change, conflicting measurement,
account incident, sensitive claim or expensive experiment. Routine formatting,
version indexing and already-authorized production should not trigger a panel.
The panel produces a recommendation, not new tool authority or an invented
professional endorsement.

## Continuity, reassignment and offboarding

The successor receives the current mandate, active account refs, latest approved
masters, open releases/cases, outstanding budget reservations, exact source
links, last evidence and next decision. Pause or replace an employee without
destroying their earlier artifacts. Revoke an employee's grant when the work
ends; preserve the owner's archive and unresolved attempts. Return account
control to the client through the platform's supported ownership procedure.
Record that handoff as completed only when its receipt exists.
