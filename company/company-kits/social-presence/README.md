# Social Presence & Growth company

Public, reusable operating kit. Reviewed 21 November 2026. Client names, account
identities, commercial documents, private source files and credentials belong in
the owner's private workspace, not in this kit.

## Purpose and present scope

Build and maintain an intentional digital presence for an authorized individual,
creator, business or organization: positioning, content, distribution, community,
discovery, conversion and useful measurement. One accountable company director
owns the complete client journey; specialist teams return small, reviewable
deliverables through Donna.

This kit supplies the organization, role instructions, record contracts and
acceptance criteria. Daftar already provides persistent companies, offices,
departments, employee definitions, shared-service contracts, knowledge
references, scoped goals, roadmap items, whiteboards and bounded worker queues.
Those are the foundations to instantiate. The marketing records and external
social adapters described below are an implementation contract: a guide or a
company record does not mean an account is connected, a scheduler is running,
a post was published, an ad was bought or a client approved the work. See the
[current platform capability matrix](platform-capabilities.md) and
[Daftar's current operating limits](../../../project/DAFTAR_MANAGEMENT.md).

## One organization, several client operating modes

| Mode | Primary outcome | Scope and operating choices |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Private individual | A deliberate profile, useful archive, professional visibility or an intentionally small presence | Owner chooses identities, desired audience and excluded topics. Personal records and private conversations stay out of general research context. Publicity is optional. |
| Creator or independent expert | A repeatable original series, audience relationship and sustainable conversion path | Connect an approved voice guide, source/rights register, production cadence and owned website or mailing list. Separate the person's opinions from client work. |
| Local business or small team | Discoverability, qualified enquiries, service delivery and repeat customers | Map offer, service area, contact destination, enquiry handling, response ownership and business measures before increasing content volume. |
| Brand or growing organization | Consistent channels, accountable campaigns and reliable handoffs | Separate brand, product, region and channel identities. Name one final owner per campaign and a named substitute for absence. |
| Organization group or agency portfolio | Shared production with independent client boundaries | Reuse employees through explicit contracts. Each client keeps its own assets, knowledge access, approvals, accounts, budget, reports and exit/export package. |

These modes are service configurations, not claims that Daftar currently has a
multi-tenant client portal. The enforced workspace boundary is the existing
company/knowledge scope. Use a separate company for a client whose information
must be isolated; a department name or a board filter is not an access-control
boundary. A shared production brief contains only the assets and facts needed
for its task. A group view may show summary status without copying client files.

Configure five company specialties at most initially: Presence Director, Brand
Strategist, Editorial Producer, Channel Steward and Growth Analyst. Their
existing runtime-role mappings and prompts are in [roles.md](roles.md). Donna
coordinates them and contracts shared specialists for actual jobs. A permanent
employee is a durable assignment, not a continuous paid model process. Do not
add one employee per platform, country, client and format before a real queue
requires that capacity.

The initial native setup retains the existing Research and Engineering department
IDs and adds the six specialist departments above. Client Strategy and Insights
are responsibility groups inside Research, not extra native department records.
Reuse the existing subdirector, knowledge curator, QA and blueprint roles; add
only the four missing specialties. Split Research later only when distinct queues
or ownership make the separation useful, retaining old work and reference IDs.

## Control and ownership

The human owner sets the mandate, client/account authorization and commercial
limits. The Presence Director translates it into outcomes and accepts internal
work. Donna routes bounded jobs, indexes returned evidence and escalates only
the decision that the current mandate cannot resolve. A departmental reviewer
checks the part they own; a release operator acts only through an available,
authorized adapter or a recorded manual handoff.

Use four distinct control records:

1. **Ownership:** client, brand, canonical platform account ID, person or entity
   controlling it, contact/ref and verified ownership date. Account display names
   and handles are labels; they can change and are not sufficient identifiers.
2. **Authority:** employee, company/account scope, permitted operation, content
   class, budget ceiling if relevant, effective dates and delegation parent.
   Runtime capability names must come from Daftar's supported set. Marketing
   action labels in this kit do not add executable privileges.
3. **Release authorization:** exact asset revision, caption, target account,
   audience, channel variant, intended time/timezone and applicable standing
   authorization or decision reference. Existing valid authorization should be
   reused, not repeatedly requested. A changed asset or destination requires
   re-evaluation against that same mandate.
4. **Execution evidence:** request/attempt ID, timestamp, provider response,
   external object ID and subsequent readback or manual receipt. An approval,
   queued command or accepted upload is not a published URL.

Account tokens stay in the platform's credential store. Jobs receive an adapter
reference and a description of the allowed operation, never raw passwords or
OAuth secrets. Do not share one client token across brands. The owner can remove
delegation without deleting previous work. Pause should stop new dispatch;
in-flight attempts need reconciliation and retained receipts.

## Complete service workflow

### 1. Establish the mandate

Record intended audience, offers, desired business or personal outcome, priority
channels, excluded subjects, voice, languages, location/timezone, current assets,
source permissions, available capacity and financial ceiling. For an individual,
ask whether they want visibility, reputation maintenance or privacy-preserving
cleanup; do not assume that growth is their aim. Link the actual account owner
and any existing authorization. Missing data remains unknown.

Acceptance: one brief identifies the accountable owner, measurable outcome,
starting evidence, boundaries and next review. The company can begin research,
drafting, asset organization and internal review without a live social adapter.

### 2. Audit and build the brand system

Inventory supplied posts, assets, source documents, domains, websites and
analytics exports. Keep historical variants and distinguish a source input from
an accepted final. Audit the funnel from first discovery to desired action and
delivery. Produce a positioning hypothesis, audience needs, content pillars,
voice examples, visual tokens, claims/proof bank and a list of missing evidence.
Do not turn unsent drafts, proposed revenue or old campaign snapshots into
measured outcomes.

Acceptance: every factual promotional claim points to a usable source and scope;
every reusable visual includes provenance, rights and a current master revision.
Claims concerning regulated products or sensitive services route to the client's
qualified reviewer. That reviewer must exist; an AI role title cannot certify
professional approval.

### 3. Plan a bounded campaign or content series

Choose one outcome and a manageable set of channels. Define the audience need,
offer, hook, format, distribution, destination, measure and production owner.
Select an explicit timezone and a date window rather than copying an old
calendar. Reserve production/model spending separately from advertising or
subscription money. Keep an idea backlog outside the committed calendar.

Acceptance: each calendar item links a brief, owner, expected artifact, channel,
dependency and review/release path. The plan distinguishes organic content,
paid promotion, community response and direct outreach; one does not authorize
the others.

### 4. Produce, adapt and review

Create a master idea and versioned channel variants. Preserve the intended
meaning when shortening copy. Supply captions, crop-safe designs, alt text where
supported, transcripts/subtitles, pronunciation notes where useful and source
material for the next editor. Review factual meaning, dates, logos, eligibility,
offer terms, audience, links, accessibility and brand voice in the actual export.
Do not multiply whole model conversations; provide the selected brief, source
refs, current artifact hash and relevant feedback.

Acceptance: the delivery packet includes the master, approved variant hashes,
rights/claims decisions, rendered previews, platform-specific constraints and a
single current revision. A reviewer can state exactly what was accepted.

### 5. Schedule or hand off, then reconcile

Check account identity, applicable authorization, adapter status, asset
availability, clock/timezone, limits and current authority immediately before a
side effect. If no adapter exists, export the package for an authorized person
or configured external scheduler and record `manual-handoff`; do not display
`scheduled` merely because a date is on a roadmap. Keep retries bounded. After a
timeout that might have created a post, look up the attempted result before
sending again.

Acceptance: a real scheduled operation has a durable scheduler receipt. A
published item has an external ID plus provider readback or a documented manual
publication receipt. A failed or uncertain attempt retains its artifact and
status. A multi-channel campaign can be partially published; never collapse it
to success for every channel.

### 6. Operate the community and maintain trust

Listen only through available authorized feeds or supplied exports. Group
questions into actionable cases, preserve the original context and route them
to the responsible service/product team. Draft helpful responses with a clear
owner. A public post mandate does not by itself grant authority to send private
messages, remove other people's content or make commitments for the client.
Separate ordinary criticism from security, privacy, service or safety incidents.

Acceptance: cases have a scope, source, severity, assignee, response state,
decision and resolution evidence. A proposed response is visibly a draft.
Unnecessary personal details stay out of shared knowledge and public reports.

### 7. Measure, learn and close the loop

Create a baseline before changing a campaign. Record source, metric name and
definition, unit, numerator/denominator, timezone, reporting window, extraction
time, paid/organic scope, attribution model/window and confidence. Show unknown
and unavailable values separately from zero. Avoid adding unlike reach counts
and calling the sum unique people. Separate views, engaged views, enquiries,
qualified leads, booked meetings, sales and realized revenue.

Acceptance: a report links the source data and states what the experiment can
and cannot establish. Descriptive change is not causal lift. Retain creative
versions and dates so later analysis can connect outcomes to what actually ran.
The next task comes from a decision with evidence, not from a generic instruction
to create more posts.

## Record and status contract

These are proposed typed records for a future marketing module. Until that
module is implemented, store scoped records as private knowledge/artifacts with
roadmap IDs. Do not present a Markdown table as a native transactional database.

| Record | Essential fields |
| --- | --- |
| Client mandate | Client/company ID, owner, service mode, goals, excluded topics, audience, languages, timezone, authority refs, budget refs, review date |
| Brand/account | Brand ID, client ID, platform, canonical account ID, display label, account type, owner, adapter reference, granted scopes, expiry, connection state, last check |
| Campaign/series | Goal ID, offer, audience, approved claims refs, channels, dates, KPI definitions, budget reservation, responsible employee, related project/site refs |
| Asset/version | Stable asset ID, revision/hash, source/master refs, channel variants, rights/consent refs, caption/alt text/subtitle refs, production state |
| Release item | Campaign/asset/version/account IDs, audience, timezone, intended time, authorization ref, current state, attempt IDs and receipts |
| Community case | Source/account, minimal necessary context, category, priority, response owner, proposed response ref, authorization, send receipt or manual outcome |
| Measurement | Metric definition/version, source, query/export hash, extraction time, period, segment, paid/organic classification, units, raw value, unavailable reason |
| Experiment | Hypothesis, baseline, comparison method, budget, planned duration, decision rule, observed result, limitations and next action |
| Knowledge transfer | Source artifact hash, recipient company/employee, explicit grant, summary, open issue, successor and retention/review date |

Use separate state machines for independent evidence:

| Domain | States and transition requirements |
| --- | --- |
| Artifact production | `idea → briefed → draft → in-review → approved`; revision after approval returns the affected variant to review; `superseded` keeps history |
| Delivery | `not-scheduled → manual-handoff` or `scheduled → submitting → processing → published`; `failed`, `uncertain`, `cancelled` and `removed` retain receipts; schedule and publish require actual execution evidence |
| Connection | `not-connected → authorizing → verified-read` or `verified-write`; also `limited`, `expired`, `revoked`, `error`; a successful login alone does not prove write capability |
| Source provenance | `uploaded-input`, `produced-draft`, `user-reported-action`, `verified-receipt`, `unresolved`; source status must not be silently promoted by an import |
| Outcome | `unmeasured`, `source-reported`, `observed`, `estimated`, `experiment-supported`; preserve methodology and uncertainty |

## Dashboard and one-chat interaction

The office blueprint groups the eight departments around a small shared
production area. Selecting a room filters the existing employee/work view and
Donna's visible scope. The ownership board shows who produces, who reviews and
who may release. The whiteboard links goal → campaign → asset → channel release
→ measure, using the same IDs rather than separate copies of the task list.

The next marketing-specific screen should have a persistent client/account
selector, date window and timezone, followed by four tabs: **Presence**,
**Production**, **Calendar**, and **Results**. Presence exposes account connection
and ownership. Production exposes current artifact revisions. Calendar shows
real scheduling status. Results separates reported data and verified outcomes.
The preview beside Donna shows the exact channel/account/audience before a
release operation, and a visible receipt afterward.

Reuse the [Daftar studio color system](../../../project/STUDIO_COLOR_SYSTEM.md):
violet surfaces, electric-green action accents and restrained golden hierarchy.
Heat colors describe a selected metric such as open work or deadlines, with
labels and units. Green decorative styling never means an account is connected
or a post is published. Unknown, stale and blocked states remain distinguishable.

The [canonical reusable catalog](../index.json) creates **ten** planned marketing
roadmap records. Their stable keys are the source of truth for the installed
public kit. The eight stages further below are an implementation guide for
operating and extending the company; they are not eight additional automatically
installed milestones or a claim that the native marketing module already exists.

| Catalog key | Installed milestone / relevant guide stage |
| --- | --- |
| `scope` | Client/account ownership register → source and client setup |
| `evidence` | Inherited project and asset reconciliation → source and client setup |
| `brand` | Positioning, audience and channel briefs → first production cycle |
| `access` | Account and integration capability verification → first supported connector |
| `calendar` | First content production cycle → first production cycle |
| `distribution` | Publishing and community handoffs → scheduling and operations |
| `measurement` | Measurement baseline → measurement and learning |
| `growth` | Bounded growth experiment → measurement and learning |
| `reputation` | Account/reputation response exercise → scheduling and operations |
| `handoff` | Client report and next cycle → scale and offboarding |

Foundation records are established by the installer. Typed marketing records and
durable scheduling are future product implementation work; an operator can add
scoped implementation milestones as needed without claiming the catalog already
supplies those adapters. Retain the canonical keys and owner's edited progress
when updating a company.

Order is dependency-based; dates are assigned after scope and capacity are known.

| Stage | Owner | Deliverable and completion evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Foundation | Presence Director + Knowledge | Create/reuse the company, office, eight departments, named specialties, service contracts, roadmap and linked whiteboard. Read back IDs and save an idempotent application receipt. No duplicate client import. |
| Source and client setup | Brand Strategist + Account Trust | Import the supplied register as private evidence, preserving its uncertainty/status distinctions. Link each client workstream and current master; keep client data out of public templates. |
| First production cycle | Editorial Producer + Creative | Produce one complete bounded campaign packet with source-linked copy, approved brand assets, accessible variants, revision history and an accountable reviewer. Export remains usable without a social API. |
| Marketing record module | Backend + Database/Cache + QA | Persist the typed records with company/account scope, revision checks, search and export. Test cross-company denial, stale revisions, asset/authorization mismatch and partial campaign state. |
| First supported connector | Engineering + Account Trust | Implement one justified adapter, then verify identity, read scope, exact release scope, token expiry/revocation, payload limits and receipt reconciliation using an authorized test target. Keep unavailable features visibly unavailable. |
| Scheduling and operations | Channel Steward + Engineering | Implement durable schedule receipts, current-authority checks at dispatch, retry reconciliation, timezone/DST handling, pause/cancel semantics and a manual fallback. Test restart and ambiguous-write recovery. |
| Measurement and learning | Growth Analyst | Import a real baseline with definitions, verify destination tracking, report one actual cycle and connect an evidence-backed next experiment. Do not attribute results to assets that were never published. |

## Shared work with the Fintech & Hackathon Studio

Contract this company for an approved fintech demo narrative, pitch deck,
landing page, product walkthrough, accessible visual exports and post-event
case study. The studio supplies exact product behavior, source facts, event
requirements and a working build receipt. Marketing supplies audience/message
fit and traceable creative. Shared employees retain their identities; neither
company inherits unrelated private knowledge. Financial claims require the
relevant source and review; a demo or prize submission is not a live regulated
service or an achieved customer outcome.

## Operating cadence and efficiency

The director sets a weekly priority list; the editor batches briefs and the
analyst returns a bounded learning review after meaningful observations exist.
Use event-driven jobs for new briefs, reviewer feedback, confirmed publications,
expired scopes and completed measurements. Quiet accounts do not justify
continuous model polling. Select the actual source segments required for a task,
cache only reusable non-secret material under company scope, invalidate on
artifact/voice/source revision and retain the evidence used for a decision.

Each job returns the [common role envelope](../../role-library/result.schema.json),
plus relevant campaign/asset/account IDs, artifact refs, status, evidence,
remaining questions and one next owner. Model spending, connector subscriptions,
media spend and client commercial figures are separate ledgers. A cap blocks new
admission while preserving work and uncertain reservations. The kit itself
starts no recurring jobs and spends no advertising or provider budget.
