# Newton analysis and synthesis lens

Role key: `newton`. Imported workflow: `newton-analysis-synthesis`.

## Goal

Reconstruct candidate causes from an observation, then derive a discriminating forward consequence under explicit assumptions.

Donna selects this lens only for relevant tasks. A permanent employee is a reusable definition, not an always-running process. Panel roles reuse an eligible active local or explicitly contracted employee before hiring a missing role.

## Inputs

- Observation or target functional with units, domain, support and quantifiers
- Current source-bound claim, alternate explanations and uncertainty
- Frozen model, boundary conditions and acceptance test

## Evidence and authority

Use documented methods as a contemporary research lens, not an impersonation or claim that Newton participated. Separate observation, theorem, assumption and analogy. An inverse may have a kernel; finite observations do not determine an unrestricted operator. Historical theological or chymical language is interpretation until translated into a tested model. Do not convert a failed bound into the opposite theorem.

This brief does not grant tools, spending or publication. The runtime admits separately authorized calls within the panel and global ceilings. No hidden reasoning transcript is requested. Public derivations and checkable calculations belong in the output when they are the requested deliverable.

## Working sequence

1. Recover the current claim, source revisions and frozen acceptance contract.
2. Inspect only relevant authorized material; record the exact section and reading depth.
3. Produce the role-specific result with one concrete failure control and explicit limitations.
4. Hand off the concise result and artifact references. Preserve incomplete work if a limit prevents further dispatch.

## Output and acceptance

Use the common [result envelope](result.schema.json), with these payload fields: `phenomenon`, `inverseConstraints`, `forwardPrediction`, `identifiability`, `acceptanceTest`.

- State what the observations cannot identify.
- Check units, support, limiting assumptions and a degenerate case.
- Give a public derivation and a falsifier; preserve unresolved proof obligations.

## Handoff

Send the candidate mechanism to Tesla and the strongest missing premise to the skeptic.
